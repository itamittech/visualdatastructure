## VIDEO UPLOAD CHECKLIST

When uploading to LinkedIn:

1. **Upload Video**
   - Drag & drop MP4 file
   - Wait for processing
   - Check preview looks good

2. **Add Captions**
   - Click "Edit" on video
   - Upload SRT file OR
   - Type captions manually
   - Review timing

3. **Write Post**
   - Copy template above
   - Customize with your URL
   - Add 8-12 relevant hashtags
   - Include clear CTA

4. **Preview**
   - Check how it looks on mobile
   - Verify captions are readable
   - Test video plays automatically

5. **Schedule or Post**
   - Use best times listed above
   - OR schedule with LinkedIn's tool
   - Post immediately if optimal time

6. **Engage After Posting**
   - Reply to comments within 1 hour
   - Ask follow-up questions
   - Thank people for sharing
   - Respond to DMs
