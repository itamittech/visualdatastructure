## STEP 3: Record (15 minutes)

### Using OBS Studio (Recommended):

1. **Download OBS**:
   - Mac: `brew install --cask obs`
   - Or: https://obsproject.com/

2. **Quick Setup**:
   - Open OBS
   - Click "+" under Sources
   - Add "Window Capture"
   - Select your browser window
   - Click "Start Recording"

3. **Record Your Video**:
   - Open VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md
   - Find your chosen video
   - Follow the shot list
   - Don't worry about perfection!
   - Do 2 takes if you want

4. **Stop Recording**:
   - Click "Stop Recording"
   - Video saves to ~/Videos folder

### Using Loom (Even Easier):

1. Install Loom Chrome Extension
2. Click Loom icon → "Screen Only"
3. Select your browser window
4. Click "Start Recording"
5. Follow the shot list
6. Click "Finish"
7. Download video (has watermark on free tier)

---

## STEP 4: Edit (30-60 minutes first time)

### Download CapCut:
- https://www.capcut.com/
- Free, easy to use, auto-captions

### Quick Edit Process:

1. **Import Video**:
   - CapCut → New Project
   - Import your OBS recording

2. **Trim to Length**:
   - Cut out mistakes
   - Speed up slow parts (1.2x)
   - Aim for 30-60 seconds

3. **Add Text Overlays** (2-3 key points):
   - Click "Text" → "Add Text"
   - Type: "Stack = Last In, First Out"
   - Make it BIG (size 48+)
   - Position: Bottom third
   - Animation: "Pop" or "Fade In"

4. **Add Captions** (CRITICAL):
   - Click "Captions" → "Auto Captions"
   - Language: English
   - Click "Generate"
   - Review for errors
   - Style: White text, black background

5. **Export**:
   - Click "Export"
   - Resolution: 1080p
   - Frame rate: 30fps
   - Format: MP4
   - Click "Export"

---

## STEP 5: Post to LinkedIn (5 minutes)

1. **Go to LinkedIn**:
   - Click "Start a post"

2. **Upload Video**:
   - Click video icon
   - Upload your MP4
   - Wait for processing

3. **Copy Post Text**:
   - Open LINKEDIN_VIDEO_POSTS.md
   - Find template for your video
   - Copy text
   - Replace [Your Platform URL] with your URL

4. **Post**:
   - Click "Post"
   - You're live! 🎉

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### "My video is too long"
- Speed up sections (1.5x)
- Cut out pauses
- Focus on core concept only

### "I made a mistake in the recording"
- Do another take (takes 5 minutes)
- OR just cut it out in editing
- OR keep it - authentic is good!

### "Captions are wrong"
- Edit them in CapCut
- Click each caption → fix text
- Takes 2-3 minutes

### "I don't know what to say"
- Use the voiceover scripts in VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md
- OR skip voiceover entirely
- Just use text overlays + captions

### "Will people watch?"
- First video: Maybe 50-100 views
- But that's 50 people who didn't know about your platform!
- It compounds: Video 10 might get 5,000 views
